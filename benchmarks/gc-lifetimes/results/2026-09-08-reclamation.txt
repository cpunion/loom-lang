collection; it is not merely eligible in principle. Post-GC live bytes are not
peak allocation bytes (a payload grows to 8 MiB between collection samples).

Loom roots. Allocator/high-water effects are a plausible explanation, not a
measured claim about the host allocator's exact release behavior.
